# Raftaar (رفتار) — Brand Identity & Design System

## 1. Brand Essence
* **Name:** Raftaar (Urdu: رفتار — Speed, Momentum, Velocity)
* **Tagline (English):** Faster than your cravings, closer than your corner store.
* **Tagline (Urdu):** آپ کی دہلیز تک، تیز رفتار اور بھروسہ مند۔
* **Values:** Reliability (قابلِ اعتماد), Speed (تیز رفتار), Hyper-local (مقامی), Respectful.

## 2. Visual Palette
Designed for high contrast, vibrant Pakistani market energy, and WCAG AA accessibility:

* **Primary Emerald (`#059669` / `emerald-600`):** Freshness, Pakistani identity, trust, and organic groceries.
* **Accent Saffron / Gold (`#F59E0B` / `amber-500`):** Warmth, delicious cooked food, Biryani tones, and celebratory deals.
* **Dark Slate (`#0F172A` / `slate-900`):** Deep contrast typography, night-mode interfaces, and premium surfaces.
* **Neutral Sand (`#F8FAFC` / `slate-50`):** Clean backgrounds that let food photography shine.
* **Alert Crimson (`#E11D48` / `rose-600`):** Low inventory, order cancellations, and emergency alerts.

## 3. Typography
* **Latin:** Inter, System-UI, sans-serif — clean, legible at small viewport sizes on low-resolution smartphones.
* **Urdu / Arabic Script:** Noto Nastaliq Urdu / System Arabic fonts for elegant right-to-left rendering.

## 4. Audio Cue Identity
* **Merchant Order Chime:** Distinctive double bell ring (approx 880Hz → 1320Hz) alerting kitchen staff of incoming orders.
* **Rider New Offer Ping:** High-pitch melodic alert signifying an urgent 30-second countdown acceptance opportunity.
* **Delivered Chime:** Harmonious gentle completion chime.
